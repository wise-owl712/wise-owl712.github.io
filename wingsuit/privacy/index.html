1. 한눈에 보기

  • 게스트로 플레이하면 운영자는 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 기록과 설정은 기기 안에만 저장됩니다.
  • 회원 가입(Google 또는 Apple 로그인)은 선택입니다. 가입하면 상점, 온라인 랭킹, 실시간 함께 날기, 클라우드 저장을 쓸 수 있고, 이를 위해 아래 정보를 처리합니다.
  • 광고는 이용자가 원할 때만 봅니다. 게임에는 버튼을 눌러야 나오는 보상형 광고만 있고(강제로 뜨는 광고나 배너는 없습니다), 광고는 Google AdMob이 제공합니다. 광고를 볼 때 Google이 광고 식별자 등을 처리합니다(9장).
  • 분석 도구나 추적 도구는 쓰지 않습니다. 운영자는 개인정보를 팔지 않습니다.
  • 크레딧 충전 같은 유료 상품은 선택이며, 결제는 Google Play 또는 App Store가 처리합니다. 운영자는 카드 번호 같은 결제 수단 정보를 받지 않습니다.
  • 게임 안에서 언제든 계정과 모든 정보를 바로 삭제할 수 있습니다(계정 삭제 안내).

  • 회원 정보는 계정을 삭제할 때까지 보관하고, 삭제하면 바로 파기합니다.

9. 광고와 자동 수집 장치

게임에는 이용자가 버튼을 눌러야만 나오는 보상형 광고만 있습니다(비행 뒤 크레딧을 늘리거나 무료 크레딧을 받을 때). 광고는 Google AdMob(Google LLC)이 제공합니다.

  • 처리하는 정보: 광고를 불러오고 보여 줄 때 Google이 광고 식별자(Android 광고 ID, iPhone의 앱 공급업체 식별자), IP 주소, 기기 정보(기종, 운영체제, 언어), 앱 정보, 광고를 본 기록과 누른 기록을 처리합니다. 운영자는 이 정보를 받지 않습니다.
  • 목적: 광고 표시, 광고 성과 측정, 부정 클릭 방지. 지역과 동의에 따라 이용자의 관심사에 맞춘 광고(맞춤형 광고)가 나올 수 있습니다.
  • 수집 시점: 광고 버튼을 누를 때만 광고 SDK가 시작되며, 광고를 보지 않으면 광고 관련 정보는 처리되지 않습니다.
  • 동의: 유럽 경제 지역(EEA), 영국, 스위스에서는 처음 광고 버튼을 누를 때 Google의 동의 화면이 나오고, 이용자가 고른 대로 맞춤형 광고 여부가 정해집니다.
  • iPhone: 게임은 앱 추적 투명성(ATT) 허가를 요청하지 않으므로 다른 앱과 웹사이트를 넘나드는 추적에 쓰이는 광고 식별자(IDFA)는 사용되지 않습니다.
  • 거부·관리 방법: 광고를 보지 않으면 됩니다(게임의 모든 기능은 광고 없이 쓸 수 있습니다). Android는 설정 → Google → 광고에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있고, iPhone은 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → Apple 광고에서 맞춤형 광고를 끌 수 있습니다.
  • Google의 정보 처리: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

게임은 이 밖에 쿠키나 분석·추적 도구를 사용하지 않습니다.

WingSuit Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

WingSuit ("the game") is a mobile game by Birch (자작나무; business registration no. 141-20-02587) ("we"). This policy explains what personal data the game processes, why, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

At a glance

  • Playing as a guest, we collect no personal data.
  • Signing up with Google or Apple is optional; members get the shop, online leaderboards, flying live and the cloud save.
  • Ads are only ever the ones you choose to watch: rewarded ads behind a button (no pop-up or banner ads), served by Google AdMob, which processes an advertising identifier and similar data when you watch one (see Ads).
  • No analytics or tracking tools; we never sell data.
  • Paid items (such as credit packs) are optional and paid through Google Play or the App Store; we never receive card or other payment details.
  • You can delete your account and all data in the game at any time (account deletion).

Guests

We collect nothing personal. Best runs, flight paths for races, credits, items, settings and today's ad count stay on the device and are removed with the app. A guest who watches an ad sends Google the data described under Ads.

Members (with consent at sign-up)

  • Google account identifier, email address and Google profile name: identifying you, keeping you signed in, verifying deletion requests.
  • Apple account identifier and email address (Apple's relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), on iPhone: the same purposes.
  • Nickname you choose: shown on leaderboards and while flying live.
  • Country code, preset from your device's region setting (no GPS) and changeable by you: shown as a flag on leaderboards and while flying live.
  • Run results (score, flight time, rings, crashes, game version, time): daily, weekly league and all-time leaderboards.
  • Flight path (a replay of your run): other players can pick you as a duel opponent and race it.
  • Reward claim records: so each leaderboard, league and duel reward is paid once.
  • Game progress (credits, items, stars, suit upgrades, achievement counters): the cloud save, so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchase records (product ID, Google Play or App Store order/transaction ID, time granted): delivering what you bought, never granting one payment twice, refunds and support.
  • Flying live: while a member flies a ranked course, their nickname, country, suit colour and in-game position are relayed in real time to other members on the same course. Relayed only, never stored; can be turned off in the settings.
  • Connection logs (such as IP address and time) may be kept briefly by our service providers for security and troubleshooting. We do not collect advertising IDs, contacts, photos, GPS location or card or other payment details (an advertising ID is processed by Google when you watch an ad and never reaches us).
  • Feedback: what you write in "Feedback" on the main menu is sent to us and stored on the server with the app version, phone model, OS version and game language, plus your member identifier if you are signed in (guests stay anonymous). Only we can read it; we use it to reply and improve the game, and a member's feedback is deleted with their account.

Public data

Nickname, country code, results and flight paths are visible to all players. Email and Google or Apple profile names are never shown.

Retention

Member data is kept until you delete your account and is deleted immediately when you do; backup copies are removed on the provider's backup cycle. Data Google processes for ads is kept under Google's privacy policy.

Sharing and processors

We do not share personal data with third parties, except where required by law. Processors: Supabase, Inc. (USA) stores and relays member data on servers in Seoul, South Korea (the provider may access it from abroad for operations); Google LLC (USA) handles Google sign-in, Google Play payments and the rewarded ads (Google AdMob); Apple Inc. (USA) handles Sign in with Apple and App Store payments on iPhone. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Ads

The only ads are rewarded ads you start by tapping a button (more credits after a flight, or free credits), served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • What is processed: when an ad loads and plays, Google processes an advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID; on iPhone the identifier for vendor), IP address, device information (model, OS, language), app information, and which ads were shown and tapped. We do not receive this data.
  • Why: showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud. Depending on your region and consent, ads may be personalized to your interests.
  • When: the ad SDK starts only when you tap an ad button; if you never watch an ad, no ad data is processed.
  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent screen appears the first time you tap an ad button, and your choice decides whether ads are personalized.
  • iPhone: the game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the IDFA used for tracking across other apps and websites is not used.
  • Your choices: simply don't watch ads (every feature of the game works without them). On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone you can turn off personalized ads in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The game uses no cookies, analytics or tracking tools beyond this.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your data. Rename: account (top right of the main menu) → "닉네임 변경". Delete: account → "계정 삭제" twice; the account, nickname, results, flight paths, reward records, cloud save and feedback are deleted immediately (account deletion). Ad choices: see Ads. Other requests, or deletion without the game: email us and we will act within 7 days.

Security

HTTPS everywhere, row-level access control so members can only read and change their own data, no passwords (Google or Apple sign-in only), minimal data.

Children

Signing up is for ages 14 and over and is confirmed at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14 and delete it if we learn of it. Younger players can play as guests, in which case we collect no personal data.
